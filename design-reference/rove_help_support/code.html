Frequently Asked Questions

Your ROVE profile serves as an intentional canvas for your creative work. Follow these steps to reorganize your pinned perspective:

Step 01 Navigate to Profile Click your avatar in the lower left dock or select "Profile" in navigation.
Step 02 Select "Edit Profile" Update your custom handle, external links, aesthetic statement, and custom header art.
Step 03 Pin Perspectives Hover any post and choose "Pin to Gallery" to lock up to 3 marquee items at the top.

Click the primary "Create Post" action button in the left sidebar. Drag and drop high-resolution JPG, PNG, or ProRes MP4 files. You can assemble multi-image carousel spreads (up to 10 visual panes), apply fine-grain vignette filters, specify original creator audio credits, and organize them directly into public or private Collections on your profile.

Head to Settings > Security & Credentials. For password changes, enter your current password followed by your new phrase (minimum 12 characters recommended). If updating your primary login address, ROVE issues a temporary 6-digit confirmation token to both the legacy and prospective inbox before finalizing the shift.

Toggle Private Profile under Settings > Privacy & Boundaries. When enabled, only approved tastemakers can view your media portfolio, inspect stories, or browse bookmarks. You can also independently restrict comments to "People You Follow" or disable public comments per publication.

Click the three horizontal dots (•••) present on any profile banner, reel card, or direct message thread. Select Block Account or choose Report to Safety Editorial. Reports are triaged 24/7 with strict confidentiality under our zero-harassment doctrine.

Visit Settings > Notifications. Here you can granularly throttle desktop push banners, set quiet hours during which alert chimes are muted, and choose between a daily or weekly Sunday editorial digest of trending collections in your niche.
