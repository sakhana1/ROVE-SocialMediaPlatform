info How Blocking Works

Blocking on ROVE is silent and unilateral. When an account is on your blocklist:

  • visibility_off Portfolio Visibility: They cannot view your galleries, stories, or published editorial collections.
  • mark_chat_read Direct Messaging: Existing message threads are archived. They cannot ping or call you.
  • label_off Mentions & Tags: Your username will not autocomplete when they type @elena_rove in descriptions.
  • notifications_off Zero Notifications: ROVE never notifies members that they have been placed on a blocklist.
